# Being Marketive — Brand Guide

- **Brand name:** Being Marketive
- **Tagline:** Marketing Playbooks for Every Industry.
- **Supporting phrase:** Learn. Apply. Grow.
- **Primary keyword:** Digital Marketing
- **Audience:** students, creators, marketers, entrepreneurs, and business owners.
- **Topics:** industry-specific strategies, SEO, social media, viral content, AI tools, paid ads, website marketing, and marketing fundamentals.
- **Voice:** practical, actionable, clear, educational, professional, encouraging.
- **Avoid:** exaggerated claims, fake proof, vague motivational filler, keyword stuffing, and guaranteed-result promises.

## Color palette
- Deep Navy Blue: #06285B
- Royal Blue: #075FE4
- Bright Blue: #078BFF
- Cyan: #18C5F5
- White: #FFFFFF
- Light Blue Background: #EEF6FF

Use colors only when visual/design guidance is requested. Keep layouts readable, high-contrast, and uncluttered.
